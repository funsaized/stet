# EVIDENCE-08 — Publish evaluation and decide continuation
## Outcome
Make continue/pivot/maintenance decision from observed use. **Parent:** F6 and 30/60/90 gates. **State:** BLOCKED/human-led.
## Prerequisites
Relevant evidence tasks and follow-up windows complete.
## Required reading
Product stop criteria, raw evidence, public signal limitations.
## Allowed edits
Evaluation report and decision record; publication requires authorization.
## Exact change
Separate fresh execution, replay, first attempt, correction and retained use.
## Acceptance criteria
Sample/limits explicit; simulated users excluded; stars/raw downloads secondary; thresholds applied honestly.
## Verification
Independent evidence-to-conclusion review.
## Forbidden scope
Cherry-picking, unsupported market/model claims, publishing without approval.
## Stop conditions
Stop if required follow-up data is missing; report incomplete instead.
## Review evidence
Decision with evidence links and dissent/uncertainty.
